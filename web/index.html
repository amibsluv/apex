FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Who can take part?

Students from grade 5 to 11. Each school can send up to 3 students in one subject, and a student can compete in up to 2 subjects.

How is scoring kept fair?

Every participant gets an anonymous code. The jury sees only the code, never the student's name or school.

How do I advance to the next round?

Reach at least 50% (general schools) or 60% (lyceums, gymnasiums, Presidential schools). From the regional round, the best 4 + 4 students per subject go to the final.

Can I appeal my score?

Yes, within 24 hours after the results are published, if you scored more than 40 points. One appeal per round.

How are places given?

90-100 points: 1st place, 75-89: 2nd place, from the pass mark: 3rd place. Other finalists receive a Certificate of Honor.
